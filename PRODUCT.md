# Requirements

- Run Felucca synthesis/sequencing on the ANN BlackBox, not on the phone.
- Preserve an accessible standalone subset of controls.
- Expose every FM-1 panel control remotely, including held keys/buttons and
  simultaneous encoder gestures, without stealing ordinary MIDI notes.
- Support computer USB MIDI first; Android USB host/OTG next.
- Route an optional Q mini through the host; do not imply device-to-device USB.
- Treat USB audio capture and analog output verification as separate capabilities.
- One supervised initial installation, then logged, bounded automatic iteration.
- No claim of reliable recovery until tested on the user's unit.
